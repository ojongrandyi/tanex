# Homepage service card photos

Six photos for the "Our services" cards on the homepage (owner's reference design, 2026-11-09).
Until a file is here, its card shows the brand panel with the service icon.

| File | Card | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| air.jpg | Air transport | cargo aircraft or airport loading |
| road.jpg | Road transport | truck on the road |
| warehousing.jpg | Warehousing | warehouse shelves or pallets |
| maritime.jpg | Maritime transport | container ship or port |
| rail.jpg | Rail transport | freight train |
| local.jpg | Local delivery | courier on a motorbike |

Rules: TanEx's own photos are best (consent from anyone shown). Otherwise free-licence photos only
(Pexels or Unsplash licence, or CC0), with **no other company's logo or livery** clearly visible,
so nothing suggests TanEx owns aircraft or ships it doesn't (tracker #121). At least 1000 px on
the short side. Record each one below, then run:

    node scripts/optimize-images.mjs --only svc-air,svc-road,svc-warehousing,svc-maritime,svc-rail,svc-local
