Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to track a shipment?

No. Enter your 8-character tracking ID (for example TX7K2M9P) on the Track page. Personal details are hidden for privacy.

Where do you deliver in town?

Our riders deliver within Buea, Limbe and Douala, for example from one Buea neighbourhood to another. Choose your city and areas when you book to see the price.

Can I follow my rider live?

Yes. Once your parcel is picked up, your tracking page shows the rider's position on the map until it's delivered.

How can I pay?

Pay in cash to the rider, or by MTN Mobile Money or Orange Money to the TanEx company accounts shown when you book. Other payment options are available for international shipments.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We ship worldwide by air, sea, rail and road, and prepare the export documents with you.

Can I write to you in French?

Yes. Our team works in English and French.
